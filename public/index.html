GUIDE MODE

01 / CONNECT

Connect a Deriv demo Options account. Keep the account token private.

02 / ANALYZE

Load a market and analyze closed candles. UP and DOWN are technical directions; WAIT means the setup did not pass the filters.

03 / REVIEW QUOTE

A proposal shows the contract price and payout. Review the details before confirming a demo purchase.

04 / MANAGE RISK

Use the daily loss cap, trade cap, cooldown and emergency stop. Never increase stake to recover losses.

05 / MEASURE REAL RESULTS

Portfolio Process uses settled ledger records only. Compare win rate with net P/L: a high win rate can still lose money if average losses exceed average wins.
